01Problem

  • Small game teams need hundreds of 3D assets. Most have no dedicated 3D artist.
  • Doing it in-house means Blender or Maya: ~3 months to learn, ~$10K/seat in hardware and licences, crashes that lose work.
  • Desktop 3D tools are single-user. Teammates wait in line for one file.
$180–1,000per asset from a freelancer, incl. iterations, and 24+ hours of waiting. Vi3W: $19/mo for up to 100 assets.

02Why now

  • Open 3D models got good in 2025. TRELLIS and Hunyuan3D made usable meshes from one image. A solo founder can now build what took a studio.
  • WebGPU ships in every major browser. Real 3D editing runs without installs, licences or a gaming PC.
  • More games, same artists. Steam releases rose from 14,111 (2023) to 19,008 (2025), mostly from small teams.

04AI edge & moat

  • Model-agnostic pipeline. Each step routes to the best model (Hunyuan3D, TRELLIS, Flux, Nano Banana). Better models make Vi3W better, not obsolete.
  • Data: sketch-to-model pairs from every generation. Individuals opt in to training. Company work is never used without consent.
  • Distribution: 3M+ organic views and a 1,000-member Discord at $0 paid acquisition.
  • Lock-in (next): one share link pulls the whole team in. Projects, versions and approvals live on Vi3W.

Why not a wrapper? Generators output a mesh. Vi3W owns the work around it: sketch input, editing, export and team review. Swap the model, keep the workflow.
